Manager’s advice: nine cases

With your group, for each case decide:
  1. Which motivation theory fits the case?
  2. What should the manager do?
1

A retail company raises pay and renovates the staff room after complaints. A few months later the complaints have stopped, but staff only do the bare minimum.

2

Sara, a junior analyst, gets bored with routine tasks. She volunteers for the hardest projects and keeps asking how well she did.

3

A call-centre manager tells her team, “Please try to improve customer service this quarter.” Three months later nothing has changed.

4

Two junior consultants do the same work with the same experience. One finds out that the other earns 15% more. She starts leaving early and stops volunteering.

5

A delivery planner uses an app that dictates every route, stop and break. She says, “I feel like a robot.”

6

A company’s reward for its top performer is a place at an annual golf tournament. Most employees do not play golf and shrug.

7

In a furniture factory, each worker drills holes all day and never sees a finished chair. Staff turnover is high.

8

Marco, a hotel receptionist, turned down a better-paid job working alone from home. He says lunch with the team is the best part of his day.

9

A data analyst prepares a monthly report but never hears whether anyone reads it or whether it helped.
